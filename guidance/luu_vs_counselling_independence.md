# LUU is independent of the University; Counselling and Wellbeing is a University service

Source: https://students.leeds.ac.uk/ (redirect target of the
`ses.leeds.ac.uk/download/downloads/id/1610/...` URL originally provided,
which now forwards to this page)

> Leeds University Union offers a professional, confidential help and
> support service. They can help you with any problems you face in your
> life as a student – no problem is too small or trivial. Although the
> team specialises in housing, money and academic concerns, they can
> support you with anything. All their help is free and completely
> independent of the University.

Source: https://www.leeds.ac.uk/studentsupport

> Independent advice from Leeds University Union (LUU): Leeds University
> Union (LUU) offers free, confidential and independent advice. If you
> need help with academic, housing, financial or wellbeing concerns, you
> can access support through student-run helplines and online community
> groups.

**Honesty note:** both sources explicitly call LUU's advice "independent."
Neither source uses the literal phrase "University-run" for Counselling and
Wellbeing. What's directly sourced is the contrast: the same
`studentsupport` page lists "Mental health and wellbeing support" (no
independence claim, presented as one of the University's own services on
its own page) immediately alongside "Independent advice from LUU" (explicit
independence claim). The internal/external distinction in this project is
built from that contrast, not from an explicit "Counselling is University-run"
sentence.
