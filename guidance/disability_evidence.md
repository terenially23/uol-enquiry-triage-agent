# Disability Services: registration requires supporting information

Source: https://students.leeds.ac.uk/support-disabled-students

> To get support, you will first need to register with Disability Services.
> To do this: Complete the Disability Services sign-up form, Provide
> supporting information about your disability.

**Honesty note:** the source says "supporting information about your
disability," not literally "diagnostic report / healthcare letter." The
agent's `missing_info.evidence_attached` field and the "diagnostic
report/healthcare letter" phrasing in prompts/write-up are this project's
reasonable gloss on "supporting information," not a direct quote. What's
directly sourced is the harder, more defensible fact for this prototype:
registration (and therefore any adjustment) requires providing something
first — an enquiry alone is not enough to arrange support.
